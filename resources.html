Guidelines, tools, and trackers worth your time. Everything here comes from a medical society or a government health agency.

⚠️ Hypertensive crisis — 180/120 mm Hg or higher

Recheck after five minutes of rest. Call 911 immediately if a reading that high comes with any of:
• Chest pain or shortness of breath
• Weakness, numbness, or difficulty speaking
• Sudden vision change or severe headache
• Back pain

Call 911 or go to the emergency department
